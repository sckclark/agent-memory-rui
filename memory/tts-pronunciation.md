任丘 is pronounced rén qiū (二声). The cloud TTS reads it as rèn (四声). In every `voice` field and every tts input, write **人丘** instead of 任丘 (text/subtitles keep 任丘).

**Why:** 郑佳蕙 pointed it out in the 蕊元书院 group on 2026-11-24 after the 小瑞狮 promo video; the video was re-voiced the same day.
**How to apply:** before sending voice to 郑董 or making narration, replace 任丘→人丘. Other likely traps: 鄚州 (mào), 雄安 fine. See [[deliverables]].
