- **润蕾** is the company behind the 北京西山国家森林公园 art shop and 西山植物商店; use 润蕾 as the partner name.
  - Value to 书院: Beijing customer flow.
  - Their gains: corporate gift orders, workshop fees, styling fee, artist residency, content.
  - They proposed placing 20万 of goods. Advised against it: start with a first batch of 3–5万 across 100+ small items; high-value art as samples plus order-and-ship; display fee or sales guarantee for the first 3 months; 0–15% commission; 书院 bears damage and insurance; 3-month pilot.
  - Copy their stall lighting and product density, not the stall shell. Market stalls only for outdoor monthly markets.
- **文创 corner (11-19):** the goal is atmosphere, not gift-box sales. Consignment, 100+ SKUs at 10–100元, 3000K lights, a stamp station and "书院信筒", rotation per 节气. Target 冬至 opening.
- **文创小店 (11-22, sea-world model):** exclusive items from 5 sources: building, 瑞狮 → mascot 小瑞狮, 六艺, 天地人和 seal, 节气.
  - First batch of 5 SKUs, about 1.5万: magnets ×3 (25–30元), 3D building keychain 39, seal mug 68, 六艺 T-shirt 99, display 2,500. 润蕾 handles high-price consignment.
  - Selling ideas: room mug "喜欢可以带走", DIY 拓印.
  - Cautions: copyright on the 瑞狮 wall and the building; trademark classes; food-grade glaze.
  - Doc: 《文创小店方案_v1》.
- **马老师** (pronouns unknown): a 研学 activity DESIGNER with about 1万 followers (Beijing parents). Not a 天坛 venue holder.
  - Advice: 书院-own age-tiered 研学 products (fills the 亲子 gap); start with model B (马老师 recruits and runs it; 书院 charges per 人·天 plus a revenue share); first run 寒假.
  - Reverse 研学: 书院 as the 任丘 招生点. 书院 must not be the organiser (needs a licensed agency, insurance, minors' safety).
  - Offered a winter-camp draft (not confirmed).
