- **No dynasty style, ever** (宋式园林/宋式建筑/宋式美学/唐宋…). **Why:** the design team said the building was not built as 宋式; "宋式" came from unverified word of mouth (owner, 11-18, corrected 11-19). **How to apply:** describe only what exists: 国学主题精品酒店, 庭院景观, 国学展厅, 茶室. Use "以传统文人的雅致生活为灵感" for the design idea. Quoted Trip.com/豆包 text that says 宋式 must be marked 不准确.
- **Not a 园林**: there is one 景观 only.
- **Ad law:** no 最好/第一/顶级/天花板. **Why:** I once recommended "任丘最好的酒店" and had to remove it. **How to apply:** use 任丘高端酒店 / 任丘酒店推荐 / 任丘书院酒店; cite rankings with platform and date ("沧州市高档酒店人气榜第1名" only with source and date).
- **雄安:** only as a location reference ("雄安旁"), never as a weekend source of guests (people go back to Beijing). Target 京津 drive-in guests. 研学 is where 雄安 content fits.
- **Don't lead with 任丘** in taglines (low recognition). Keep 任丘 in the address, platform first lines and keywords.
- **Don't raise the 携程 phone-number item again.** The owner deleted it: it's a 携程 restriction and out of our control.
- All amenities are provided in the room (decision): no 扫码索取 card.
- The design brief's "非遗", 五行药膳 health claims and named universities (清华/人大) must not appear unless verified or approved. Planned experiences are publicised only once they actually run.
- 小红书 posts: blur or hide children's faces; don't write "孤困儿童".
